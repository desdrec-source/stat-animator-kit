# Style notes (summary)
These are short, original descriptions of the six styles in this kit.
- **dark_stat_card**: dark background, bold condensed headline, donut chart and stacked bars in bright accents. Built for social stat posts.
- **editorial_paper**: warm paper tone, serif headline, restrained palette with a single red accent, thin rules. A newspaper data-desk look.
- **highlighter_newsprint**: textured newsprint, a marker highlight behind the key number, and hand-drawn circle annotations. An explainer look.
- **kinetic_big_number**: one huge counting number with a colour flash, then supporting facts. Built for Shorts and Reels.
- **neo_brutalist**: flat bold colour blocks, thick black borders, hard shadows, stepped motion.
- **terminal_market_desk**: monospace font on near-black, green and red ticker colours, dense data rows.
